### 5a. The noise fix and its stopping rule

The rule, replay figures, stopping rule, counters, refit and gold-versus-rubric disclosures, and held-out cost are verbatim in the record's `noiseFix`; this section says what they commit to.

- **One stopping rule, stated once (`noiseFix.stoppingRule`).** t3 is the last tree on which a noise-motivated change is judged. The spend proceeds only if the labelled Q is ≤ 4 (pooled and scripts, point estimate) and no dev item other than the four named is newly unlisted; otherwise it stops and the stakeholder chooses between deferring criterion 3 to P2 and not spending, with no automatic fix or new tree. A pooled Q below 1.49 is a disclosure, not a stop. The t3 labels are frozen once complete (a relabel only under the spot-check floor, larger Q used); any later tree counts as exposure 4 under the same rule.
- **Counters (`noiseFix.fixCounters`).** Noise-motivated fixes: 1, cap 1. Dev-noise trees: t1, t2, t3, cap 3, t3 the last.
- **What a t3 pass is.** A refit statistic from two LLM roles, chosen after reading the t2 labels; label stability, not validity. No held-out noise measure exists; "noise not measured" stands.
- **Gold versus rubric.** Disclosed beside the Q with the gold-aware sensitivity figures, never in its place; A resolves the tension in the rubric's favour at the gold's expense (`noiseFix.goldVersusRubric`).
- **Held-out cost of A**, one Poisson model, estimates not measurements (`noiseFix.heldoutCost`): about 2 expected held-out key-error-class misses, P(at least one) about 0.865, a 7.4-fold cut in pass chance (about 0.35 to 0.05). The stakeholder was told these figures and on 2026-11-25 still chose A (`noiseFix.stakeholderAcceptance`: the ledger entry of that date; the requirements-log entry is owed).
- **Cost audit** of the dropped items is not run before the spend; only after t3 is recorded, offline, blind and descriptive, with no result changing A or the rule (`noiseFix.costAudit`).
