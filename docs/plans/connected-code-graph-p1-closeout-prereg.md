- **Floors:** 75 pooled, 8 per gated stratum, over the scored set. Pre-marker floor refusal: when the exclusion removed at least one counting item and the kept pooled counting items are under 75 or a kept gated stratum holds under 8 items, `run` exits 5 before the lock, the build and the marker; the list is not spent; counts only on stderr. At the cap neither can occur (pooled at least 104, scripts at least 48: arithmetic from the counts), so the refusal guards a miscount.
- **Cap:** 17 or more of the 64 counting scripts items withheld refuses the spend. Closed outcome set: (a) leave the list unspent and carry AC-5 open, or (b) one fresh draw of scripts items in this unit, at most one (new blind draw, new record, shape count and cap recomputed, the redraw and its count reported; the over-cap list's scripts items may not return as candidates; builders get no shape-letter count of either draw before its ask). Raising the cap, narrowing or editing the predicate, or building a shape and spending the same list after seeing the count are excluded.

The closed outcomes above stand; a redraw's supply is not established by this record, and the count of fresh scripts items available goes in the ledger line.

- **Two names, not to be confused.** The harness `--gate-id` of every `gate_run` verb at this record, the rehearsal included, is the stratum id `ac5-gate-1-r2`. The EP §11.2 noise-draw label is `ac5-gate-1-dev-t2` (t2 = the tree ordinal: a new F or record starts a new one), seed `ccg-noise-v1|ac5-gate-1-dev-t2|<snapshotId of the first run at the tree>`. A rerun at an unchanged tree reuses the same seed and labels, so it cannot re-roll the sample; bar 3 is judged on the largest Q across reruns at the tree.
- **Sample:** a pooled draw over all dev answers (at least 100 possible items, EP §11.2) and a scripts-only draw (at least 60, min 5 per stratum, census if fewer; 60 is a labelling-load choice, not a power figure), so the dev scripts answers have their own estimate.
- Both labellers are LLM agent roles: 18 of 20 agreement is not independent validation, and the labelled Q is a dev fit statistic that carries nothing about held-out noise.
- **Bar 3 is the observed point estimate** Q ≤ 4 (EP §11.3), with the bootstrap interval reported: about 23 dev scripts answers (one new anchor at t2 may carry 15 gold items and a large listing) make the percentile bootstrap poorly calibrated, and an upper-bound rule would be unpassable.
- A missing labelled Q reads NOT MET. A labelled Q_scripts (or pooled Q) above 4, the largest across reruns, is a rehearsal miss; the iteration count and any resolver tuning against dev are disclosed. A first labelled Q above 4 is a live outcome, not a tail. Block A reports the largest-answer share at t2 beside Q. A new tree (t3) re-draws the sample by design, but unchanged (item, answer) pairs reuse their t2 labels, so a fix cannot buy a new Q on the same answers.
